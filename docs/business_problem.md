# Business Problem

## Company Context

**Robinhood** offers a free trading account to all users and an optional paid tier called **Robinhood Gold**.  
The marketing team runs paid campaigns across four channels to acquire new users and convert them to Gold subscribers.

---

## Stakeholder & Decision

**Stakeholder:** Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)  
**Decision at stake:** Setting the H2 2025 (July–December) paid marketing budget allocation.

---

## Problem Statement

Over H1 2025 the marketing team spent **$124,886** across four paid channels and six campaigns.  
However, the CMO has observed that raw signup volume does not tell the full story:

- Some campaigns attract many free signups that never convert to paying Gold subscribers.
- Some campaigns produce fewer signups but a disproportionately high share of paying customers.
- At least one channel stopped running partway through the period with poor results.
- Unpaid channels (organic search and referral) show strong conversion rates at no direct media cost.

The CMO needs a rigorous, data-driven analysis to answer:

> **"Which channels and campaigns are actually worth the money — and how should we reallocate the H2 budget?"**

---

## Scope

| In scope | Out of scope |
|----------|-------------|
| H1 2025 (Jan 1 – Jun 30) | Prior or future periods |
| Four paid channels: Paid Search, Paid Social, Display, Affiliate | Other company marketing activities |
| Two unpaid benchmarks: Organic, Referral | Email, push notifications, in-app campaigns |
| 90-day revenue window per user | Full customer lifetime value |
| Channel / campaign-level analysis | Creative-level or audience-segment analysis |

---

## Success Criteria

The analysis is successful if it can answer all eight business questions in the README and produce a specific,
quantified budget recommendation backed by at least three KPIs.

---

## Business Questions (ranked by importance)

1. Which campaigns produce paying subscribers most efficiently (lowest CAC)?
2. Which campaigns produce the best return on each dollar of spend (highest ROAS)?
3. Where is money being spent with no proportionate conversion outcome?
4. How do paid channels compare to free channels on conversion quality?
5. What are the month-over-month trends — are things improving or deteriorating?
6. Where should H2 budget go?
